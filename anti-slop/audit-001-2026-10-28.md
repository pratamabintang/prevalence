### 2. WCAG AA Contrast Failure on `success` Button Variant (`2.45:1`)
- **Location**: `src/app/shared/ui/button/button.component.ts:34`, `src/styles.css:9`
- **Rule / Skill**: `antislop` R-28 / `antislop-human` (Color Contrast)
- **Why it is slop**: White text (`#FFFFFF`) over `#4AB5B5` yields a `2.45:1` contrast ratio, failing WCAG AA (`4.5:1` minimum for normal text).
- **Resolution Applied**: Replaced `#4AB5B5` with `#0F766E` (`5.47:1` PASS on `#FFFFFF`, verified via `contrast-check.py`) and updated `--color-brand-teal` in `src/styles.css`.

### 3. Low-Contrast Secondary Text & Micro-Typography (`8px-10px` on `slate-400`/`slate-500`)
- **Location**:
  - `src/app/shared/ui/navbar/navbar.component.ts:46, 130`
  - `src/app/shared/ui/badge/badge.component.ts:20-34`
  - `src/app/shared/ui/page-header/page-header.component.ts:27`
  - `src/app/features/home/home.component.html:196-306, 614-1210`
  - `src/app/features/chart-visualizer/chart-visualizer.component.html:621, 1103-1331, 2473-2952`
  - `src/app/features/stunting-monitoring/stunting-monitoring.component.html:87, 115, 143`
- **Rule / Skill**: `antislop` R-28 / `antislop-ui` (Typography Scale) / `antislop-human` (Contrast)
- **Why it is slop**: `text-slate-400` (`#94A3B8`) on white (`#FFFFFF`) is `2.56:1` (FAIL), and `text-[8px]`/`text-[9px]` badges are illegible on standard office monitors.
- **Resolution Applied**: Upgraded secondary labels and subtitles to `text-slate-600` (`#475569`, `7.58:1` PASS) or `text-slate-700` (`#334155`, `10.35:1` PASS) and raised micro-badges to `11px-12px` minimum. Unhid `ui-page-header` subtitle on mobile viewports.

### 4. Missing `tabular-nums` on Numeric Columns, KPI Cards, and Axis Labels

- **Rule / Skill**: `antislop-ui` (Tabular Numerals)
- **Why it is slop**: Proportional numerals jitter horizontally when filtering districts, switching quarters, or comparing ranked tables.
- **Resolution Applied**: Added global `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` rules in `src/styles.css` for `table`, `th`, `td`, `.tabular-nums`, `.font-mono`, and `svg text`, plus explicit `tabular-nums` classes on KPI cards and ranking lists.

### 5. Suppressed Focus Rings (`focus:outline-none` without `:focus-visible` Replacement)

- **Rule / Skill**: `antislop` R-29 / `antislop-human` (Keyboard & Focus States)
- **Why it is slop**: Removing focus outlines traps keyboard users without visual feedback.
- **Resolution Applied**: Added a global `:focus-visible` rule (`outline: 2px solid #525aff; outline-offset: 2px`) in `src/styles.css` and `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-[#525AFF]` across buttons and interactive controls.
